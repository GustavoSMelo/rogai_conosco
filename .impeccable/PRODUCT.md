# Rogai Conosco

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is the Christian community — people of faith who seek prayer support or want to intercede for others. They may not belong to a local church, may feel shame about their circumstances, or may simply need a prayer that extends beyond their existing social circle. The secondary audience is anyone going through hardship who needs a safe, anonymous place to ask for prayer, regardless of their relationship (or lack thereof) with the faith.

## Product Purpose

A safe place to ask for prayer anonymously. This project exists so that no one has to face their struggles alone — every request is met with dignity, respect, and real prayer from real people, not algorithms.

## Positioning

Anonymous prayer from real people, not bots. The only platform where your prayer request is received, carried, and answered by another human being who takes the time to pray for you personally — with zero judgment, zero tracking, zero strings.

## Conversion & proof

- **Primary CTA:** Request a prayer (the visitor's core action — asking for a recorded, instant, or AI-led prayer)
- **Line to remember:** "Someone is praying for you."
- **Belief ladder:**
  1. I can share my need here without anyone knowing who I am.
  2. A real person of faith will receive my request, not an algorithm.
  3. They will pray for me with sincerity, time, and attention my need deserves.
  4. I don't need to belong to any church or denomination to be worthy of prayer.
  5. This space is sustained by love and donations — it will never charge for prayer.
- **Proof on hand:** None yet. The project is new. Trust is earned through the design itself — calm, transparent, reverent, with no dark patterns.

## Brand Personality

Peaceful, trustworthy, humble. The voice is quiet and assured, not loud or pleading. The site should feel like sitting in a sunlit room with someone who listens without interrupting. Compassion without pity. Strength without bravado.

## Anti-references

- No generic SaaS or startup aesthetics — no corporate dashboard vibes, no sterile blue buttons, no "growth" language.
- No flashy megachurch or TV evangelist excess — no gold, no dramatic hero shots, no prosperity gospel imagery.
- No dark, gothic, or overly somber moods — the space should be peaceful, not heavy or intimidating.

## Design Principles

- **Anonymity as safety** — the design must never betray trust. No tracking feels, no "your account" surfaces that suggest surveillance. Transparency in every interaction.
- **Peace over hype** — calm, quiet, unhurried. Nothing shouts, pulses, or begs for attention. The rhythm is contemplative, not urgent.
- **Humble confidence** — beautiful but not flashy. Crafted but not expensive-looking. The kind of care that says "someone thought about this" without saying "look at what we spent."
- **Mobile-first, midnight-ready** — someone at 2 AM on their phone in a dark room needs this to work. Large tap targets, readable text, minimal cognitive load.
- **No barriers to blessing** — readable, simple, clear. No sign-up walls, no gatekeeping, no friction between the visitor and their prayer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Standard contrast, focus states, readable fonts, screen reader support. The design should serve all ages and all levels of familiarity with technology.
